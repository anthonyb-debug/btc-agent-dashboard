What the terms mean

Total calls
Inbound calls plus outbound calls.
Handle time (AHT)
Average handle time: (talk time + hold time) ÷ total calls. The sheet has no handle-time column, so the dashboard calculates it.
Talk time
Time spent speaking with callers, from the sheet.
Hold time
Time callers spent on hold, from the sheet.
Inbound time
Time spent on inbound calls, from the sheet. Shown in the tables only.
Shift
AM is the morning shift; PM is the midnight shift. Filtering by shift limits every number to that shift.
